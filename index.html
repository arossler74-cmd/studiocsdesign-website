The studio

Warm, layered rooms shaped around the pieces you keep and the way you actually live.

From a mood board to a finished room

Every project moves through five steps, from the things you love to the room itself.

  1. Gathering what you love

    A fig, a violet dusk, dried flowers in late light. We start with pictures, not furniture, and draw a palette from them.

  2. Choosing colours and materials

    Oak, walnut, linen, cane and brushed bronze, chosen by hand against that palette.

  3. Drawing the plan

    Every wall, opening and piece of furniture drawn to scale, so you know what fits before anything is ordered.

  4. Shaping the room

    A white model shows the volumes and the daylight before a single finish goes on.

  5. Finishing the room

    The materials go on, the pieces arrive, and the room is yours.
